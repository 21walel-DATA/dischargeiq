The three stages of the Discharge Medicines Service

  1. 1. Review the discharge information

    AI reads the letter; plain code compares it with the pre-admission list. Aim to finish within 72 hours of the referral.

    Fee £12

  2. 2. Check the first new prescription

    The same comparison checks the GP's first prescription against the discharge list, so a missed change stands out.

    Fee £11

  3. 3. Talk to the patient or carer

    Record the consultation, then copy a claim-ready summary into Manage Your Service.

    Fee £12

AI only reads

It pulls medicines out of the letter and quotes the sentence each came from. It never suggests a dose or a switch.

Code compares

The started, stopped and changed list comes from tested rules, not from AI. If a match is uncertain, the line is marked for review.

You approve

A stage can't be completed until you've checked every flagged line and added your initials.
